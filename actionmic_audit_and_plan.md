# ActionMic AI Project Notes

## Current product

ActionMic AI is a meeting recording and intelligence workspace. The active product is centered on meeting records, media processing, speaker-aware transcription, and extracted meeting outcomes.

## Supported workflows

- Online meeting recording
- Offline meeting recording
- Video upload with audio extraction
- Audio upload and processing

## Processing pipeline

1. Create a meeting record.
2. Capture or upload media.
3. Normalize media and extract audio where required.
4. Transcribe and identify speakers.
5. Generate summary, key points, decisions, action items, and follow-ups.
6. Display the result in the meeting workspace.

## Product direction

Keep new UI, API routes, schemas, and services under the meeting domain. Retire older consultation-oriented screens and APIs as compatibility work is scheduled.
